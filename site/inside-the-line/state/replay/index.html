The state fills

A filling model on 10-metre ground. It ranks towns; it does not map streets. A place is wet at a level if it lies below the level and the sea can reach it over ground that is also below it. No waves, no rivers in flood, no drains, no pumps. Providence's barrier is not in this ground: the water is drawn as if the gates stood open.
Dark blue is land the water has reached; the darkest edge is what this half foot added. Feet above NAVD88, one level for the whole coast. A real storm is not level: the marks above the bar are where Sandy (2012) and the 1938 hurricane stood at Newport, the marks below it at Providence. The water runs higher up the Bay.
