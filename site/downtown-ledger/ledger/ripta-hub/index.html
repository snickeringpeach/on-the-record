RIPTA transit center relocation

Proposed Stage: proposed

Newest evidence 2026-10-06.

On August 20, 2026 RIPTA's board voted 6 to 0 to take a new transit center into design, engineering and pricing on a 4.4-acre state-owned parcel facing Francis and Gaspee streets, next to Providence Station and across from Providence Place. Contractor Next Wave Partners. Kennedy Plaza would keep reduced service via the Downtown Transit Connector. The governor's schematic shows 16 bus berths curving along the Francis and Gaspee intersection, west of the station and south of the State House. RIPTA will own the site; it is a tenant at Kennedy Plaza, which the City owns. Next Wave's contract is about $16.9 million. The board packet puts the preliminary estimate at $70 million, not final, against $77 million committed ($46 million in the state transportation plan, a $25 million federal BUILD grant, $6 million state match), and says construction would begin within six months of finishing the design contract. No date is given for finishing it; the design and a cost estimate come back to the board first. RIDOT's station page still says 2026 for the station work next door, with no completion announced as of Oct 6. RIPTA's project page, checked Oct 6, gives no date or cost beyond the August 20 vote and says site investigations are under way. S5 S16 S16b S27 S28 S29 S45

On the list because the city has proposed it. Not yet verified by the author.

Years out

4 years out

An estimate, not a stated date: design, engineering and permitting of about a year and a half, construction starting within six months of that per RIPTA's own packet, and about two years to build.

What it needs first

  • Design, engineering, pricing, public engagement and permitting complete, and the proposal with a cost estimate returns to the board for approval; no date stated S5 S16 S29
  • Construction contract after board approval S5
  • Funding assembled: 2014 transit bond share plus a $25M federal BUILD grant, with state documents describing the bond figures differently ($20.5M recommended, $22M issued) S16b
  • Route restructuring settled: which routes move and when S16b

Notes

The buses route to the Francis and Gaspee frontage by the State House and the station. Opposition on record: Kennedy Plaza Resilience Coalition; a former board member urged delay pending a service analysis.
