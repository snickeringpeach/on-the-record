Housing units inside

877 housing units on the line, 2020

Newest evidence 2026-10-06.

877 housing units in 2020 inside the line (Census PL 94-171). Since then, about 141 units have opened inside the line, all in converted older buildings: the Lapham and Trayne buildings at 290 Westminster (52, move-in from September 2020), the Studley at 86 Weybosset (65, 2023, 14 deed-restricted) and the Lauderdale at 136-144 Westminster (24, about 2025; opening month not found). That would put the line near 1,018 units, an estimate. The assessor's file (early-2025 extract) shows no new residential construction inside the line since 2020. Not started: the Superman building at 111 Westminster (308 units, start expected 2027, waiting on a federal loan) and the old Providence Journal building at 203 Westminster (115 units, financing unresolved as of January 2026). The Woolworth (185 Westminster, 16) and Gaspee (204 Westminster, 5) conversions are on record but no completion was found. Just outside the line: Dyer Wharf, 200 Dyer St (214 units, approved, end of 2027 targeted).
