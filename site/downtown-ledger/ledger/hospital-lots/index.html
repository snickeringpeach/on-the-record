Hospital parking lots in the Jewelry District

Proposed Stage: proposed

Newest evidence 2026-10-06.

Brown University Health holds 4,280 surface spaces including off-site, more than 40 acres of it in the hospital district. The City Plan Commission approved its Institutional Master Plan in May 2026: the main entrance moves to Plain Street, a 500-space garage is built, Plain and Dudley reconnect. While the garage is built, the hospital will park on the Victory Plating site in the Jewelry District. Marathon dropped its separate seven-story garage at 140 Prairie Ave on April 28, 2026 after neighborhood opposition, moving the parking into its 251 Blackstone St building, which was cut from six stories to four; it says 140 Prairie is now for a future residential and parking hybrid. The March 2025 CNU charrette's 2,000-unit proposal on consolidated lots is a proposal, not a plan of record; the approved plan says nothing about housing. S14c S14d S14e R32 R34
