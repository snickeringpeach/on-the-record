Newest evidence 2026-09-28.

Downtown-specific: more than 20% of commercial space empty in the Financial District and nearly 32% in Capital Center, per the Boston Globe in April 2025. Q4 2021 was 15.85% for downtown per CBRE, the last published downtown-only rate. Market-wide: 13.7% overall vacancy, all classes, Q2 2026, per Cushman & Wakefield and Hayes & Sherry's Providence MarketBeat; year-to-date net absorption 115,000 sq ft; asking rent $23.03. The report says the market is tight with no speculative office construction expected, and that FM's purchase of 10 Memorial Blvd took nearly 60,000 sq ft of Class A space off the central business district.
