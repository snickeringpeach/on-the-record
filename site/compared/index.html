When a dam fails: Michigan, 2020

After the Edenville and Sanford dams failed in May 2020, restoring the four lakes was put at $398.9 million. After state and other offsets, $217.7 million is being assessed on property owners around the lakes2223. Downstream residents’ suit against the state was dismissed on April 23, 2026; the court found the private owners, not the state, controlled the dam24.

Per household is our arithmetic, and the denominator is a choice. Providence: occupied households in the 2020 Census blocks inside the barrier’s levee-credited area, from Inside the Line; the barrier protects more jobs and tax base than homes. Miami Beach: households, 2020–24. Dare County: households and housing units; the sand protects the units, 54 percent of which are nobody’s household. The 1966 cost is scaled to today by the consumer price index, about tenfold. Press figures of $400–600 million for Miami Beach’s program are not reconciled with the city’s $103 million and are left out.

An island buys cash, not protection. Jamaica’s parametric insurance and catastrophe bond paid about $242 million within weeks of Hurricane Melissa (2025)4445, against damage the World Bank and IDB put at $8.8 billion46: about three cents on the dollar. Puerto Rico after Maria was obligated $35.6 billion in FEMA public assistance, about $22,700 per housing unit [MODEL]47; its power authority had spent $2.7 billion of $11.1 billion by December 202548.
