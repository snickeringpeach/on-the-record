Providence’s 311, on the Record

What the City’s service-request system publishes, what its form asks of a resident, and who files. Counted from the City’s own site.

The front door

The “New Request” button on PVD311’s home page leads to a sign-in screen. A resident without an account can report through “Guest Support,” which is in the site’s menu but not among the buttons on its home page. The guest form takes no photo attachments.

The menu

To file, a resident picks one of 128 case types from a single alphabetical list, from “Abandoned or Vacant City Owned Property Concerns” to “Zoning Violation Concerns.” 15 of the descriptions tell the resident to call someone else, such as the police non-emergency line, Rhode Island Energy or Providence Water. One type is a catch-all, listed as “I do not know know how to classify my issue or service request.”

The last seven days

PVD311 shows a rolling seven-day list of cases on its “Public Requests” page. On 2026-10-07 it showed 206 cases, created 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-07. That is about 29 a day, against the year’s pace of about 76 a day, so the public list appears to be a subset. Which cases it shows is a question for the City.
