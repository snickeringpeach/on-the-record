    What this record is

    These are the crash reports Rhode Island police agencies filed with the Department of Transportation, as RIDOT produced them under the state's public-records law after the General Assembly made crash data public in June 2026. They come from records request 26-373, released October 6, 2026: every reported crash from January 2015 through mid-September 2026, with a count of the bicyclists involved in each. A crash is on this map if that count is one or more.

    Where the dots are

    The map draws each crash at the location RIDOT recorded. For 292 of the 3,016 crashes that location is missing or falls outside Rhode Island; those are counted but not drawn.

    Deaths are a floor

    Injury severity is coded at the scene and not revised, so someone who dies in the hospital days later can still appear here as a serious injury. Against the federal count of cyclists killed (NHTSA, Fatality Analysis Reporting System), this file matches in 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022 and 2023, records one fatal crash in 2020 where the federal count has two deaths, and two in 2024 where it has five. Read the fatal category as at least this many.

    Two versions of the same file

    An earlier RIDOT extract, from records request 26-286, held none of these bicycle crashes and runs about 6,000 to 8,000 fewer crashes a year overall than 26-373. The difference is not yet accounted for. This map uses 26-373 only.

    What it leaves out

    Only crashes police reported are here. Falls, near misses and collisions no one reported are not. The record has no field for bike lanes or other bicycle facilities, so this map cannot say whether a crash happened in one.
