HOUSING · NEW

Providence permitted 778 new homes in 2025, or 324, depending on which government count you read

The State wants 3,010 over five years. Its count of permits and the Census Bureau’s disagree by more than two to one.

RENT STABILIZATION · NEW

The rent stabilization ordinance would reach roughly 60 to 65 percent of Providence’s rental homes

Not the fewer than 44 percent the City’s finance chief told the Council. A parcel-by-parcel count from the City’s own records.

BY WARD · NEW
