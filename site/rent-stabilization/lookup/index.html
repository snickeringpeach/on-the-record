What the answer rests on

  • The 2025 property tax roll (City open data, dataset 6ub4-iebe) for the address and the City’s owner-occupancy code; the Assessor’s parcel database for unit count and year built; HUD’s Multifamily database for federal project-based subsidy.
  • Owner-occupied buildings of 1 to 4 units are exempt (13-78(n)); so is a small landlord’s second building of 1 to 4 units, matched on owner name, so that test is approximate.
  • New construction: buildings the Assessor dates 2022 or later are treated as exempt (13-78(m)), assuming a 2027 effective date. Year built is the database’s weakest field.
  • Not screened: condominiums the parcel database doesn’t match, apartments in commercial or mixed-use buildings, and state-subsidized buildings other than 8 Law.

This is an estimate for a first look, not a legal determination; the ordinance’s text and the City decide. The full count · the data behind this page (JSON).
