# Horizons series — Providence, on the record

Liam's idea, Nov 3 2026: a series posing the key questions for the city (and to some extent the state) over 3-month, 6-month, 9-month, 12-month, 2-year and 4-year horizons.

**Structure (Liam's call):** one piece per topic, each run across 1 to 6 of the horizons.

**First piece:** what Providence doesn't tax (exempt parcels, PILOTs, tax stabilization agreements). See ~/Projects/untaxed.

Candidate later topics: housing / rent stabilization; institutional expansion (Brook Street, Brown); transit (RIPTA); city budget and taxes; flood and climate (hurricane barrier); 311.

---

## Piece 1 — What Providence doesn't tax

Question frame below is Claude's draft (approved as structure Nov 3); specifics to be checked against records before publishing.

- **3 months** — What's on the books: how many exempt parcels, which PILOTs and TSAs are in force, what each pays, what the city forgoes vs. full taxation. Who keeps the list, and why does it take a records request? Hook: Woonsocket (Nov 3 2026) has no annual reports or certifications for any of its TSAs; if nobody checks compliance there, does anyone here? *(Spine: everything later depends on having the ledger.)*
- **6 months** — Budget season: does anyone put the forgone revenue next to the shortfall? Does the state's PILOT reimbursement to the city get funded at the statutory level?
- **9 months** — Tax bills go out: who absorbs what exempt/stabilized parcels don't pay — homeowners, renters via rent, commercial owners. (Who-pays.)
- **12 months** — Which agreements expire, step up, or come up for renewal; who negotiates, on what terms; does the council see them before signing?
- **2 years** — Did the big institutional agreements deliver what was promised? Any TSA being challenged (cf. Woonsocket 43 Railroad St — Madison Leigh Properties v. City of Woonsocket, 1:26-cv-711, D.R.I.)?
- **4 years** — As institutions expand (Brook Street), how much of the city's land is off the roll, is the share growing, and what would a sustainable rule look like?

Calendar anchors to verify: GA session Jan–Jun; governor's budget Jan; city + state budgets May–Jun; fiscal year Jul 1; state elections 2028; mayoral cycle 2030.
